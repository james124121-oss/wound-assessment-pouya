9. Export & Google Drive Backup

Export the current patient/wound assessment locally, or save a structured copy to your Google Drive through your own Google Apps Script endpoint.

Google Drive

Configure the Apps Script endpoint first. The included google_apps_script.gs file is ready to deploy.

What is exported?

Patient identifiers, hospital/MRN/admission data, wound location/type/procedure, baseline and follow-up dates, wound area, granulation area and percentage, wound-area reduction, healing rate, burn-depth fields, TBSA values, clinical notes and generated note. The anonymized research export excludes name, phone, national ID and MRN.

Safety & intended use

Prototype for clinician-supervised documentation and measurement. The burn-depth assistant is a heuristic decision-support feature based on standard clinical burn characteristics (colour, pain, blanching, blisters/surface and sensation) and is not a validated diagnostic classifier. It does not diagnose infection, ischemia, flap compromise, malignancy, burn depth, or other conditions. Measurements depend on accurate calibration, wound-border tracing, camera angle, and image quality. Clinical decisions require direct examination and appropriate investigations.
